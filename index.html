bakra eid

Bakra Eid, also known as Eid al-Adha, is the Islamic festival of sacrifice, commemorating Prophet Ibrahim’s devotion and obedience to Allah. Meaning and Origin Bakra Eid, or Eid al-Adha, literally means "Festival of Sacrifice" in Arabic, while the term "Bakra Eid" is commonly used in South Asia, highlighting the tradition of sacrificing a goat (bakra) during the festival. It is celebrated on the 10th day of Dhu al-Hijjah, the last month of the Islamic lunar calendar, coinciding with the culmination of the Hajj pilgrimage.
